Why I built it

The wall every outbound team hits.

Real personalization takes a rep about an hour per prospect, so it never scales, and everyone falls back on {first_name} templates. I didn't buy that this was a law of nature. I thought it was a tooling problem.

So I built the proof: one person orchestrating the right AI services can out-personalize a whole outbound team and never drop the follow-up. That system is CVP Engine, and everything below is how it works and what it's done.

01
What it does

A full outbound motion, collapsed into one workflow.

Not a tool. It's the whole motion: sourcing, research, personalization, and follow-up, running as one repeatable command.

A

Personalized at scale

Each prospect gets a video composited around their own business, not a merge tag. The kind of personalization that used to take a rep an hour, made in seconds, for everyone.

B

Run by one operator

I pick the niche, approve the copy, review a few samples, and push. The machine handles sourcing, rendering, hosting, and sending end to end.

C

Smarter every round

Replies feed back in. What lands gets reused, what flops gets cut. The next batch starts from what already worked.

02
How I built it

Seven services, one command.

I designed the workflow in Claude Code, connecting seven specialized services into a single run where each stage hands clean data to the next. I built the sourcing, enrichment, qualification, and sequencing layers; video rendering and CDN hosting were built by a technical partner.

Claude Code · orchestration

The brain

Coordinates the workflow: decides what to source, fit-checks each lead, writes the copy, hands off to the render pipeline, and pushes the finished batch into sending, with human checkpoints I kept at copy approval and sample review.

Prospeo

Sources decision-makers in the target niche with verified contact data.

Firecrawl

Scrapes each prospect's site + ads to fit-check against ICP and pull visuals.

Supabase

The system of record: leads, status, render links, send state.

Modal

Renders a personalized video per prospect in parallel, on demand.

Bunny CDN

Hosts every video on its own fast, trackable link.

Instantly

Sends from a warmed inbox pool and routes every reply to one place.

ICP gate

A qualification pass I built to block anyone off-target before a dollar of compute is spent.
