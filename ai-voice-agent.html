Why it exists

Most leads die in the gap before anyone calls back.

A lead that isn't answered in the first few minutes goes cold, and no team can pick up every call at every hour. So the fix is an agent that never misses one: it answers instantly, qualifies, and books, so a human only ever talks to someone worth talking to.

I build and install that agent for a business's exact flow, its questions, its calendar, its offer, and train it over a 30-day setup so it handles the real calls, not a demo script. Below is what it does and how it's wired.

01
What it does

Answer, qualify, book, without a human in the loop.

Not a phone tree. A conversation that carries a lead from first ring to a booked, qualified call.

A

Answers every lead, instantly

Picks up the moment a form comes in or a call lands, 24/7, so no lead sits waiting on a callback and none slip through after hours or on weekends.

B

Qualifies before it books

Runs the business's own qualifying questions and fit rules, so only ready, on-target leads land on the calendar and the team's time goes to real opportunities.

C

Hands over a warm call

Books straight into the live calendar, confirms by text, and passes the rep a lead that's already been screened and knows exactly what the call is about.

02
How it's built

Wired into the business's own stack.

The agent isn't a bolt-on bot; it's connected to the calendar, the CRM, and the qualifying rules the business already runs on.

Conversational voice AI · the system

Conversational voice AI

Qualifying logic

Calendar booking

SMS + email confirm

CRM logging

Writes the call, the answers, and the outcome to the CRM with no manual entry.

Training + handoff

A 30-day install tuning the agent on the client's offer and objections, plus training the team to run it.
