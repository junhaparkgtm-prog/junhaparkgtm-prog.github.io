Why it exists

Cold traffic doesn't show, and the ones who do don't book.

Most cold-traffic webinars show at 15% and book at 15%, so the math never compounds. The fix isn't more spend, it's making every step reinforce the last: the right person, the right message, and the right offer from the ad all the way through to the call.

So I built the funnel as one system where nothing leaks, ad to page to show to book to follow-up, each stage tuned to the exact person on the other end and measured against a benchmark. Below is how it's wired and what it did for one coaching client.

01
What it does

A full acquisition motion, tuned end to end.

Not a landing page and some ads. It's the whole motion: message-market fit, warm-up, booking, and follow-up, run as one measured system.

A

Message-to-market match by niche

Separate ad hooks for each audience, W-2 earners, nurses, near-retirement, so the person sees their own situation, not a generic pitch. The right message is what earns the click and the opt-in.

B

Warms the lead before a human calls

A founder VSL, a thank-you-page video, and an email + SMS drip carry the lead from cold click to a live webinar already believing the offer. By the call, they're pre-sold, not cold.

C

Nothing leaks between steps

Ad, page, show, booking, and follow-up all reinforce each other. Every stage sits on a tracking sheet, so a drop anywhere gets caught and fixed the same week, not a month later.

02
How it's built

Six pieces, one measured funnel.

A direct-response funnel where each stage is built to hand a warmer lead to the next, and a performance sheet watches all of it.

Direct-response funnel · the system

The spine

Paid traffic into a founder-led VSL, opt-in to a live webinar, calendar and questionnaire to book, pre-frame and drip to warm, and an enrollment call to close, with a performance sheet tracking every dollar from ad spend to net calls booked.

Meta · Google Ads

Direct-response ads with founder talking-head creative, targeted only at people who actually fit the program.

VSL landing page

A tight, single-message opt-in page; the video does the first round of selling before a lead ever registers.

Live webinar

The core mechanism: a free masterclass that teaches the model and calls out the viewer's specific opportunity.

Calendar + questionnaire

Books the call and qualifies intent, so reps spend their time on people ready to move.

Email + SMS drip

Pre-frame sequences with strong deliverability that hold the lead from registration through to the call.

Performance sheet

Daily tracking of spend, CPL, show rate, cost per attendee, and net calls booked, the control panel for the funnel.
